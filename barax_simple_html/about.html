Our Story

Barax Clothing is a South African streetwear brand associated with Limpopo. The brand developed from a small clothing venture into a growing streetwear business.

Barax represents fashion, creativity, confidence and individuality.

Our Mission

To provide bold streetwear clothing that allows customers to express their individuality and style.

Our Vision

To grow Barax into a recognised African streetwear brand while maintaining its connection to local culture.
